GVA Calculator - Basic Usage

Quick guide to modeling employment, profit, and GVA scenarios.

Calculator Assumptions

  1. GVA = salary/wage costs + pre-tax profit + depreciation.
  2. GVA due to grant: The value of GVA with grant (Scenario 2) minus the value of GVA without (Scenario 1).
  3. Salary/wage cost: An average cost per employee is calculated using the baseline figures. This average is then applied to the total number of jobs created or lost to estimate the overall labour/wage cost.
  4. Non-labour costs = turnover − salaries/wages − pre-tax profit. These costs then scale proportionally with increases or decreases in turnover over Years 1–3.
  5. Productivity = turnover / employee count.
  6. How productivity increase is applied: a target is entered for each year as % vs baseline productivity. With manual headcount, the boxes show the productivity the headcount gives.
  7. How non-labour cost saving is applied entered per year as % points of turnover for that year.
  8. Base year: from April it is the current calendar year; before April it is the previous year.
  9. Depreciation: The baseline is applied to each year, the project cost is depreciated equally over three years in sceanrio 2.
  10. Depreciation and net profit Depreciation is excluded from the net profit calculation.

Initialising Baseline Figures

Set initial Turnover, Net Profit, Employee Count, and Salary Costs. Click the Edit Baseline button in the summary panel to modify these values at any point.
